## About

I work at the intersection of **computer vision, local LLM infrastructure, and practical AI tooling**.

My research background includes collaborative work on **image super-resolution, image restoration, stereo vision, and low-light enhancement**, including papers in the NTIRE / CVPR Workshops community. Today, I focus on taking ideas beyond experiments: building systems that people can run, test, and use.

I enjoy self-hosted AI, developer tools, agent workflows, and open source.
